HOW TO EDIT YOUR PORTFOLIO WEBSITE
===================================

You only ever need to touch TWO things:

  1. js/content.js   -> all your text, contact details, projects and picture names
  2. images/         -> where you put your picture files

Everything else (index.html, css, js/main.js) is the "engine".
Please leave it alone.


THE FOLDER MAP
--------------
portfolio-website/
  index.html            (do not edit)
  HOW-TO-EDIT.txt       (this guide)
  js/
    content.js          (EDIT THIS: your words and settings)
    main.js             (do not edit)
  css/                  (do not edit)
  images/
    profile/            put your profile picture here
    covers/             put the big cover pictures here (one per page)
    projects/           put your project pictures here


HOW TO OPEN THE WEBSITE ON YOUR COMPUTER
----------------------------------------
Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.
After you save a change in content.js, press F5 (refresh) in the browser.

Tip: edit content.js with Notepad, or a free editor like Visual Studio Code.


THE 4 RULES FOR EDITING content.js
----------------------------------
1. Change only the words between the "quotes".
2. Keep every "quote" and every comma at the end of a line.
3. Do not type a double quote (") inside your text. Use a single quote (')
   instead. Example:  "I'm a fast learner"
4. If the page goes blank after an edit, you probably deleted a quote or a
   comma. Press Ctrl+Z to undo and try again.


1) CHANGE YOUR PROFILE PICTURE
------------------------------
a. Save your photo as   profile.jpg   inside   images/profile/
   (a square or portrait photo of your face works best).
b. Refresh the browser. Done.
Using a different file name? Open content.js and change this line:
   profile: "images/profile/profile.jpg",
No picture file? The site shows your initials in a green circle.


2) CHANGE THE COVER PICTURE OF A PAGE
-------------------------------------
Each page has a big picture at the top (like a Spotify cover).
a. Save wide pictures (about 1600 x 600 pixels) inside   images/covers/
   with these names:  home.jpg  about.jpg  projects.jpg  resume.jpg  contact.jpg
b. Refresh the browser.
No picture file? The site shows colorful cover art for that page.


3) ADD OR REPLACE A PROJECT PICTURE
-----------------------------------
a. Save your picture inside   images/projects/
b. In content.js, find your project and set the file name:
      image: "images/projects/my-new-picture.jpg",
c. Refresh the browser.

Every project card is the same size, whatever the shape of your picture.
   fit: "contain"  shows the WHOLE picture (best for screenshots)
   fit: "cover"    fills the whole frame and crops the edges (best for photos)
   focus: "top"    with "cover", keeps the top of the picture visible


4) ADD A NEW PROJECT
--------------------
a. Open content.js and find the "PROJECTS PAGE" section.
b. Copy one whole block from   {   to   },   (the whole project)
c. Paste it right below the last project, then change the words.
d. Put its picture in images/projects/ and type the file name after "image:".

To remove a project, delete its whole block.


5) CHANGE YOUR TEXT, EMAIL OR PHONE NUMBER
------------------------------------------
Open content.js. Section 1 (BASIC INFORMATION) holds your name, email,
phone and location. They update everywhere on the site automatically.
Everything else is grouped by page: HOME, ABOUT, PROJECTS, RESUME, CONTACT.


BEFORE YOU PUBLISH: QUICK CHECKLIST
-----------------------------------
[ ] Profile picture added
[ ] Cover pictures added (or you are happy with the cover art)
[ ] Every project has a real picture of your own work
[ ] The "Sample project" label is changed (projects > badge in content.js)
    once you have real work to show
[ ] Email and phone number are correct
[ ] You checked every page on your phone


HOW TO PUT THE WEBSITE ONLINE (free options)
--------------------------------------------
Upload the WHOLE folder, keeping the folders inside it.
- Netlify: go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the folder in.
- GitHub Pages: upload the folder to a GitHub repository and turn on Pages.
Then share the link on your resume and job applications.
